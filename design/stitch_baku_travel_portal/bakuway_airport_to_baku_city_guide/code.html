Heydar Aliyev Airport (GYD) to Baku City

Compare your three main options and expand any card for terminal pickup photos.

Best with luggage

Taxi (Bolt or Yango)

30–40 min · Direct to your hotel door

25–35 ₼ ≈ $15–21
  1. Exit Terminal 1 Arrivals and cross the first pedestrian lane to Zone B.
  2. Open Bolt or Yango instead of negotiating with terminal touts.
Open in Bolt Open in Yango
Cheapest Needs BakuCard

Aero Express Bus (H1)

40–50 min · Every 30 min to 28 May Metro

1.50 ₼ ≈ $0.90
  1. Buy a BakuCard (2 ₼ + 3 ₼ top-up) at the red kiosk right outside Terminal 1 doors.
  2. Board the H1 express bus at the dedicated bay on the right.
1 transfer

Bus H1 + Baku Metro

60 min · Reach Old City or Nizami

Under 2 ₼ ≈ $1.20

Ride H1 to the final stop (28 May), walk 2 minutes underground to the Red Line, and take 2 stops to Icherisheher (0.50 ₼).

Good to know on arrival

Last update: Nov 25, 2026
SIM cards: Azercell & Bakcell booths in Arrivals sell 10 GB tourist SIMs for ~20 ₼.
Currency: ATMs in Arrivals give fair rates; exchange only a small amount at the airport desk.
BakuCard: Keep 5 ₼ loaded for your first 2 days of metro and bus rides.
Taxi tip: Always confirm the license plate in Bolt/Yango before loading bags.
