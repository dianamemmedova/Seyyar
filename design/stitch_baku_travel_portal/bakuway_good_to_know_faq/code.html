Visa and e-visa

How do I apply for an Azerbaijan e-visa? expand_more

Most travelers apply online via the official ASAN Visa portal at least 3 working days before arrival. Standard e-visas cost $20 (+ $5 service fee) and remain valid for a 30-day stay.

Do I need to register my address if staying over 15 days? expand_more

Yes, stays longer than 15 days require registration with the State Migration Service. Hotels handle this automatically at check-in; apartment hosts can submit it online for free.

Money and currency

Can I pay by card in Baku, or do I need cash? expand_more

Visa and Mastercard work almost everywhere in Baku—hotels, restaurants, supermarkets, and Bolt/Yango taxis. Keep 50–100 ₼ in small notes for Old City craft shops, tea houses, and regional shared taxis.

Where should I exchange currency? expand_more

Exchange a small amount at Baku Airport for immediate needs, then use bank branches around Fountain Square or ATMs from Kapital Bank, ABB, or Pasha Bank.

SIM and internet

Which local SIM card or eSIM is best? expand_more

Azercell and Bakcell have the strongest 4G coverage in both Baku and mountain regions like Gabala and Sheki. Tourist SIM desks in Terminal 1 arrivals offer 15 GB plans for around 25 ₼.

Safety

Is Baku safe for families and solo travelers at night? expand_more

Yes—Baku’s Seaside Boulevard, Fountain Square, and Old City are lively and well-lit until late evening, with friendly local families strolling after dinner.

Etiquette

What should I know about dress code and tipping? expand_more

Baku is relaxed and modern. Cover shoulders and knees only when entering mosques. In restaurants, 10% is appreciated if service isn’t already listed on the bill.

Best time to visit

When is the nicest weather in Baku and the regions? expand_more

April–June and September–October bring mild 18–26 °C days ideal for walking and mountain trips. Summer is warm by the Caspian, while winter is cozy for tea houses and museums.
