Modern architecture · Museum

Heydar Aliyev Center

1 Heydar Aliyev Ave, Narimanov, Baku · 4.9 (1,420 reviews)

About this landmark

Designed by Zaha Hadid Architects, the Heydar Aliyev Center is Baku’s most recognizable contemporary building. Inside, bright flowing galleries showcase Azerbaijani carpets, musical instruments, and miniature architecture models.

Highlights

  • Permanent “Pearls of Azerbaijan” exhibition of carpets and traditional dress
  • Mini Azerbaijan hall with scale models of historic landmarks
  • Expansive green lawn ideal for families and sunset walks

Tips from locals & photo spots

Stand by the large reflecting pool on the lower terrace for the classic reflection shot, or walk up the eastern lawn at 17:00 when soft golden light hits the white curves.

Accessibility: Step-free ramps, elevators to all floors, and stroller-friendly walkways throughout the park.
