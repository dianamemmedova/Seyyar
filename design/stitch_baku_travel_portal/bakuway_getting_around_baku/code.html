Metro hours

Daily 06:00 – 00:00. Trains arrive every 2–4 minutes at peak hours.

How to pay

Tap a BakuCard or use the BakuCard QR app at metro gates and bus readers. Cash is not accepted on buses.

BakuCard price & where to buy

Plastic card costs 2 ₼ (plus top-up). Sold at Airport Arrivals kiosks and every metro station machine.
